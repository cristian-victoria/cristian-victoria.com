Whose AI harms get written down?

A pre-registered audit of the public record of AI harm. I wrote down the question, the data and the test before opening any data, then checked where the incidents in two databases actually happened: 136 coded incidents from the AI Incident Database and 1,944 located records from the AIAAIC Repository.

The record leans hard toward the US. China, the other frontier-AI country, barely appears in it. I directed AI research agents for the analysis and had a separate reviewer critique it before anything was written up. Next: multilingual news coverage.
