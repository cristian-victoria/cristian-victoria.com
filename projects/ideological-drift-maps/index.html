Case 02NLP researchOct — Dec 2025Word2Vec · LDA · GensimCode on GitHub ↗

Seventy-six years of the same words, meaning different things.

I trained word embeddings decade by decade on 40 U.S. party manifestos (1948–2024) and measured how far 18 political terms drifted, then used topic models to see which themes rose and fell.

Trade moved most after the war. Terrorism was redefined twice, first around the Cold War, then around the Middle East. Since then, political language has grown more uniform. I presented the findings to more than 20 faculty and students at a department seminar.
